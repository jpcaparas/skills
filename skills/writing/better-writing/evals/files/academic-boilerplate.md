# Draft research summary

Against the backdrop of a growing body of literature, this study aims to explore appointment delays and gain a deeper understanding of the patient journey. The findings shed light on a critical issue, contribute to our understanding of access, and open new avenues for future research.

The retrospective study compared appointment records for 312 adults at one hospital from 2019 to 2024. Median waiting time was 19 days for patients referred by primary care and 11 days for patients referred internally. The records do not contain income, transport, or cancellation data. The study cannot determine why the groups differed or whether the result applies to other hospitals.
