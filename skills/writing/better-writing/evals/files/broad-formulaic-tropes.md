# Draft council update

In today's rapidly evolving landscape, research shows that Harbor plays a pivotal role in reshaping community engagement. But here's the kicker: this is not just about convenience; it is about empowering residents and unlocking the potential of local participation. At its core, Harbor serves as a testament to the council's unwavering commitment to excellence.

Here's what's interesting. During the 12-week pilot, 84 residents submitted 137 maintenance reports through one form. Before the pilot, residents emailed one of four inboxes, and staff copied each report into a spreadsheet. With Harbor, each submission receives a reference number and appears in the same queue for assignment. The council has not measured resolution time or resident satisfaction. The pilot ran in East Ward only.

In conclusion, Harbor paves the way for a brighter future. Only time will tell, but the possibilities are endless.
