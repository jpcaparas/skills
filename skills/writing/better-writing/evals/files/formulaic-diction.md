# Approval queue pilot

The new approval queue marks a significant shift in how product and engineering collaborate. It bridges the gap between review and delivery, unlocking seamless alignment and greater visibility. This is not just about speed; it is about empowering teams to move forward with confidence.

Before the pilot, release owners copied approval decisions from three email threads into a spreadsheet. During 11 pilot releases, reviewers approved or rejected the current build in one queue, and the release owner could see which team had not responded. The pilot may not represent larger releases.
