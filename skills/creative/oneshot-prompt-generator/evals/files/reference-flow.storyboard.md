# Ember Transit launch film

Accessible frame log and audio transcript for a 00:18 product film.

## Timeline

- `00:00–00:02` — Pure black. A low rail hum fades in. One amber dot appears at the lower left and stretches into a thin route line.
- `00:02–00:05` — The route line draws diagonally through a charcoal city grid. Station nodes pulse once as the line reaches them. No camera cut; the view glides with the line.
- `00:05–00:08` — The line folds into the edge of a phone-shaped transit pass. The pass rotates 12–15 degrees into a front view with a soft overshoot. On-screen copy: `Your city, already moving.`
- `00:08–00:12` — Three quick but continuous state changes on the pass: `Plan` highlights, a route card expands, then a disruption notice shifts the route. Spoken line: “Plans change. Ember moves with you.”
- `00:12–00:15` — The phone dissolves back into the city grid. Multiple cyan and amber route lines flow without colliding. A short two-note confirmation sound lands as one route turns green.
- `00:15–00:18` — The grid dims. The Ember flame mark remains centered above `Move with the moment.` and a rounded `Join the early ride` action. The rail hum resolves into silence; the final frame holds for 1.2 seconds.

## Visual and audio system

- Background: near-black charcoal with faint cool-gray street lines.
- Primary route color: warm amber. Secondary routes: desaturated cyan. Successful reroute: clear green.
- Type: compact geometric sans with wide breathing room; sentence case except the Ember wordmark.
- Motion: continuous paths, restrained overshoot, no hard cuts, no particles, no lens effects.
- Sound: low rail ambience, one spoken line, one restrained confirmation interval, and deliberate silence under the final hold.
