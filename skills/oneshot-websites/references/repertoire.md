## Style Cards

### restaurant

- Concept: A restrained fine dining brand, not a generic restaurant landing page.
- Palette: Near black, warm cream, aged gold, and one deep wine accent.
- Typography: Elegant serif display paired with quiet sans body copy.
- Layout: Large centered hero statement, tasting-menu rhythm, reservation strip, and course-like section reveals.
- Signature techniques: CSS grain, parallax light layers, horizontal menu procession, inline SVG candle or flame.
- Avoid: Food stock imagery, punny brand names, bright red accents, oversized photo cards.

### perfume

- Concept: A niche fragrance house where the page feels like scent moving through air.
- Palette: Obsidian, amber, mist white, and a restrained rose or powder accent.
- Typography: High-contrast serif display with airy sans supporting text.
- Layout: Sparse hero, note pyramids, bottle silhouette made with CSS, and an inversion section for the signature scent.
- Signature techniques: Canvas mist particles, blur fields, floating note pills, soft scroll-linked opacity.
- Avoid: Beauty ecommerce grids, literal product photos, heavy gradients that overpower typography.

### bookshop

- Concept: A rare-books dealer whose website reads like an old ledger discovered at night.
- Palette: Ink, parchment, sepia, aged red, and shadow brown.
- Typography: Historic serif display with readable literary body copy.
- Layout: Ledger hero, catalog entries, provenance notes, marginalia, and a quiet acquisition callout.
- Signature techniques: Repeating SVG paper grain, CSS book spines, clip-path ink reveal, footnote hovers.
- Avoid: Modern bookstore cafe styling, cute illustrations, dense paragraphs without typographic hierarchy.

### deep-sea

- Concept: A fictional oceanographic station below the thermocline.
- Palette: Midnight navy, bioluminescent cyan, abyssal purple, and foam white.
- Typography: Monospace UI for instruments with a clean body face for research copy.
- Layout: Immersive descent hero, pressure/depth meter, specimen log, station modules, and mission footer.
- Signature techniques: Canvas particles with sine drift, sonar rings, scroll depth indicator, ASCII specimen panel.
- Avoid: Bright tropical ocean palettes, generic space styling, random fish icons.

### jazz

- Concept: A legendary late-night club and vinyl lounge with archival warmth.
- Palette: Near black, bourbon amber, faded cream, and one electric blue accent.
- Typography: Condensed or slab display type with literary serif support.
- Layout: Poster-like hero, set list, vinyl collection, listening room, and late-night schedule.
- Signature techniques: Procedural waveform, CSS smoke rings, vinyl hover spin, film grain, marquee.
- Avoid: Neon club cliches, equalizer overload, illegible novelty fonts.

### botanical

- Concept: A studio for rare architectural indoor plants.
- Palette: Moss, cream, terracotta, dark soil, and occasional leaf highlight.
- Typography: Organic serif display with compact geometric sans body copy.
- Layout: Greenhouse hero, specimen grid, care rituals, growth timeline, and studio visit details.
- Signature techniques: Inline SVG fronds, stroke-draw growth, earthy section color shifts, tilt-on-hover specimen tiles.
- Avoid: Beige-only wellness styling, stock plant cards, decorative leaves that do not relate to layout.

### mineral

- Concept: A geological specimen house for collectors and designers.
- Palette: Deep slate, amethyst, pyrite gold, rose quartz, and mineral white.
- Typography: Elegant geometric display with rounded body copy.
- Layout: Faceted hero, specimen rows, provenance certificates, rarity matrix, and collector appointment CTA.
- Signature techniques: Conic-gradient facets, 3D CSS specimen cards, backdrop refraction, shimmer sweeps.
- Avoid: New-age crystal shop tropes, rainbow palettes, inaccessible low-contrast text over facets.

### observatory

- Concept: A remote observatory that doubles as an educational retreat.
- Palette: Deep space, constellation silver, nebula rose, and horizon amber.
- Typography: Futuristic display kept in check by practical sans body copy.
- Layout: Sky map hero, observation schedule, journal log, instrument section, and night-program footer.
- Signature techniques: Canvas starfield, animated constellation paths, CSS moon phase, logbook cards.
- Avoid: Generic sci-fi dashboard, excessive blue-purple gradients, unreadable star backgrounds.

### night-market

- Concept: A hawker market guide and food culture magazine.
- Palette: Lantern red, turmeric yellow, night charcoal, white steam, and banana-leaf green if needed.
- Typography: Multilingual-capable sans with condensed display labels.
- Layout: Street-entry hero, stall grid, dish story cards, market map, and late-night guide.
- Signature techniques: Canvas steam, CSS lantern string, market-map CSS grid, animated stall signs.
- Avoid: Tourist brochure gloss, mismatched cultural motifs, decorative scripts that break readability.

### distillery

- Concept: A small-batch whisky or spirits house rooted in place and material.
- Palette: Peat dark, copper, aged oak, water clear, and cream.
- Typography: Classic serif display with restrained body typography.
- Layout: Heritage hero, still-room diagram, barrel age statements, flavor wheel, and batch notes.
- Signature techniques: SVG or CSS copper still, liquid pour clip path, polar flavor chart, huge age numerals.
- Avoid: Generic bar styling, overused rustic textures, illegible gold-on-brown combinations.

### architecture

- Concept: A boutique architecture practice known for light, proportion, and materiality.
- Palette: Concrete white, graphite, clay or brass accent, and pale shadow.
- Typography: Modernist sans throughout with careful scale and spacing.
- Layout: Strict grid hero, project index, material studies, process diagram, and studio footer.
- Signature techniques: One-pixel dividers, floor-plan lines, expandable project cards, measured whitespace.
- Avoid: Decorative cards, loud gradients, centered marketing sections, fake 3D building renders.

## Naming Guidance

Use names that sound plausible and restrained. Prefer "Maison Vorieux", "Atelier Obscur", "The Meridian Press", "Sublevel 7", or "Station Nuit" over jokey or literal names.
