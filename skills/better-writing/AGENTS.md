# better-writing

Canonical behaviour lives in `SKILL.md`.

Use this general-purpose skill to draft, rewrite, line edit, review, humanise, or adapt prose while preserving source truth, exact literals, uncertainty, and writer identity. Remove or rebuild canned phrase families from supported meaning; never apply a raw-token ban to precise, technical, literal, quoted, or writer-owned language. For explicit em-dash, semicolon, or colon transformations, choose sentence structure by meaning instead of replacing characters globally. Treat scanner matches as revision prompts, never evidence of authorship.
