# Renewal job pilot

During the July pilot, 46 of 50 renewal jobs finished within 15 minutes while four remained in `awaiting_confirmation` for more than 20 minutes and cleared only after support refreshed them manually, and although the provider log records a callback for each of the four jobs, the application log does not capture the callback ID, so we do not know whether the provider delayed delivery or the worker discarded the event; support spent 31 minutes on the manual checks, the current proposal is to send 25% of customers through the new path next week, and this may shorten the overnight backlog, but it may also increase recovery work until engineering can trace the callback, which is why the product lead said, “A green job count is not the same as a verified renewal,” and recommended one more internal run before customer traffic moves.
