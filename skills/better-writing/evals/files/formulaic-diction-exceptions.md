# Mixed uses of gap and shift

## Release note

This release represents a meaningful shift and closes the gap between intention and execution. Reviewers now approve the current build in the shared queue, and the release owner can see which team has not responded.

## Exact prose

Ana took a gap year before university. The carpenter left an 8 mm gap under the door. The report says the pay gap narrowed from 12% to 8%.

Jo starts the night shift at 22:00. Press the Shift key while selecting the second row. The instrument recorded a phase shift of 12 degrees at 50 Hz. The monitoring note describes distribution shift between the training and production samples.

## Protected quotation

Mara wrote, “This marks a major shift and bridges the gap between planning and delivery.”

## Defined research absence

No published trial has compared seven- and 14-day regimens in adults over 70. That is the research gap this study addresses.
