## Current issue basis

The official sources identify Issue 9, dated 15 January 2025, as the current issue. It replaces previous issues and contains:

- 53 rules in nine writing-rule sections
- a controlled dictionary
- updated terminology aligned with relevant ISO terminology work
- revised wording in 31 rules
- updates to 555 dictionary entries

The nine sections govern words, multi-word nouns, verbs, sentences, procedural writing, descriptive writing, safety instructions, punctuation and word count, and writing practices.

This package paraphrases only the compact principles necessary to route and review work. It does not reproduce the rule text, dictionary, or official examples.

## Tool boundary

The official FAQ and tools page state that:

- checking tools are optional
- no tool replaces the writer or the standard
- ASD and the STEMG do not endorse, certify, or authorize software tools, including AI tools
- tools can produce inaccurate or misleading feedback
- not all rules can be checked automatically
- governed technical terminology is necessary for useful checking
- writers remain responsible for clarity, accuracy, and compliance

This package therefore treats automation as diagnostic and requires human review for consequential approval.

## Copyright, trademark, and distribution

ASD owns the document and the registered ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English trademark.

The official PDF states that possession does not imply rights to reproduce or publish it. It gives special usage rights to enumerated organizations and institutions. The official sources make the current PDF available free of charge but prohibit unauthorized distribution.

For this public package:

- link to the official request or download route
- do not commit the official PDF
- do not embed or reconstruct the dictionary
- do not copy complete rule text or official examples
- do not use ASD or STEMG logos or cover art
- do not imply endorsement, authorization, or certification

Users who need the standard can request the free official copy through the [official download page](https://www.asd-ste100.org/STE_downloads.html).

## Compliance-claim limit

The official sources do not publish a software certification or document conformity-assessment scheme.

A defensible internal review requires, at minimum:

- the applicable official issue
- every relevant dictionary and rule decision
- correct text-type classification
- governed organization terminology
- preservation of technical meaning
- applicable safety, legal, regulatory, and publication directives
- named human review

That checklist is an audit practice derived from the official responsibility boundaries. It is not an ASD certification procedure.
