# Borrowed Weather

Borrowed Weather is a community tool for neighbours to lend weather-specific items before buying them: umbrellas, rain jackets, gumboots, portable fans, shade tents, dehumidifiers, and heaters.

The first release should help someone answer three questions:

1. What can I borrow within a 15-minute walk?
2. Is it available during the weather window I care about?
3. What condition is it in, and what does the owner expect?

## Rules

- People browse by weather need, not by product category.
- An item has an owner, approximate walking distance, condition, availability windows, pickup notes, and optional deposit.
- Exact addresses stay hidden until both neighbours accept a request.
- Requests can be accepted, declined, cancelled, collected, returned, or marked overdue.
- The service must remain useful when no nearby item matches: show the next-closest distance and let the person save the need for later.

## Voice

Practical, neighbourly, and calm. Avoid disaster language and sustainability lectures. The line `Borrow for the weather you actually have.` is approved and must remain exact.

No visual identity or implementation stack has been chosen.
