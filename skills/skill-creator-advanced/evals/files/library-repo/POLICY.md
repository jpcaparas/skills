# Example Skill Library Policy

Canonical published skills live under `skills/stable/`, drafts under `skills/lab/`, and retired packages under `skills/retired/`.

Published skills must appear in `CATALOG.md`, `registry.json`, and the `skill-map` router. Draft and retired skills stay out of those active surfaces. Renames update dependents and evals in the same change. A fresh `tool skills list` command is the final discovery check.
